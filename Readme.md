## 🎮 About

A western-themed arcade shooter where you take the role of a lone gunslinger defending the frontier from alien invaders. Point, shoot, and rack up as high a score as you can before they overrun you!

## 🕹️ How to Play

- Aim your cursor at enemies and **click to shoot**
- Each enemy you take down earns you points
- Survive as long as you can and beat your high score!
